## Overview

Lexi is a web-based educational game designed to help identify early signs of dyslexia in children aged 4–6.

The concept transforms screening into a playful experience where children complete simple tasks while the system captures behavioral data such as accuracy and response time.

The project was selected among the **Top 15 teams at the 2025 FEIT Hackathon Festival**.

![Lexi overview](/images/lexi-overview.jpg)

## Problem

Early detection of dyslexia is important for providing timely educational support. However, traditional screening methods can be difficult to access and may feel intimidating for young children.

There is an opportunity to create tools that make early screening more engaging and accessible.

![Problem illustration](/images/lexi-problem.jpg)

## User Journey

A user journey was created to map how children and parents would interact with the screening process.

The goal was to design a short and engaging experience that keeps children focused while collecting meaningful behavioral data.

![User journey](/images/lexi-journey.jpg)

## Design Process

The game mechanics were designed around sequential tasks that test skills related to early literacy development.

These tasks include activities related to:

- Visual imagery
- Numeracy literacy
- Pattern recognition

Gameplay was designed to last around 15 minutes to match the attention span of young children.

![Design process](/images/lexi-wireframe.jpg)

## Solution

Lexi presents screening tasks as an adventure-style game.

Children complete a series of simple challenges while the system records behavioral patterns. These patterns can then be analyzed by an AI-assisted model to estimate the likelihood of dyslexia risk.

The results are translated into clear feedback for parents and educators.

![Final UI](/images/lexi-ui.jpg)

## Reflection

This project demonstrated how educational games can support early detection of learning challenges while keeping children engaged.

Designing for young users also highlighted the importance of simplicity, visual clarity, and age-appropriate interactions.
