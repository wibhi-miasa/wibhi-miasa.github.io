## Overview

Pedipal is a mobile application concept designed to help parents manage their children's healthcare information in one place. The app focuses on simplifying everyday health management tasks such as tracking vaccinations, scheduling doctor appointments, and monitoring growth and developmental milestones.

The goal of the project was to create a centralized and user-friendly platform that supports parents in keeping their children's health records organized and accessible.

![Pedipal overview](/images/pedipal-overview.jpg)

## Problem

Parents often need to manage multiple aspects of their children’s healthcare, including vaccination schedules, doctor appointments, and developmental records. However, this information is frequently scattered across different sources such as paper records, hospital documents, or personal notes.

Key challenges identified:

- Health records are often fragmented across different systems
- Parents may forget important vaccination schedules
- Tracking growth and development can be difficult without clear visualization

![Problem illustration](/images/pedipal-problem.jpg)

## User Journey

To better understand how parents manage their children's health information, a user journey map was created to identify key interactions and pain points.

The journey revealed several opportunities to simplify healthcare management through digital tools, particularly in organizing health records and providing reminders.

![User journey map](/images/pedipal-journey.jpg)

## Design Process

Based on the insights gathered, several interaction concepts were explored to simplify how parents access and manage health information.

Design priorities included:

- Clear information hierarchy
- Easy navigation for busy parents
- Simple data input for health records

Prototypes were created using Figma to demonstrate the core interaction flows.

![Wireframe or prototype](/images/pedipal-wireframe.jpg)

## Solution

Pedipal provides parents with a structured interface that helps them manage their children’s healthcare more efficiently.

Key features include:

- Vaccination tracking and reminders
- Pediatric appointment scheduling
- Growth and development monitoring

The design focuses on providing quick access to essential information and reducing the complexity of managing children's health records.

![Final UI](/images/pedipal-ui.jpg)

## Reflection

This project highlighted the importance of clarity and accessibility when designing health-related applications. Parents often need to access important information quickly, so simplifying navigation and presenting health data clearly is essential.

Through this project, I learned how thoughtful UX design can support parents in managing healthcare tasks more confidently.
