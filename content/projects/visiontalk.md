## Overview

VisionTalk is a concept website designed to help users explore and understand smart glasses technology through an engaging digital experience.

The project focuses on presenting product features in a clear and interactive way so potential buyers can easily learn about the product and explore customization options.

![VisionTalk overview](/images/visiontalk-overview.jpg)

## Problem

Smart glasses are still an emerging technology, and many users are unfamiliar with how they work or how they could be useful in daily life.

Existing product pages often present overly technical information that can overwhelm users and make it difficult to understand the value of the product.

![Problem illustration](/images/visiontalk-problem.jpg)

## User Journey

A user journey was created to understand how people typically explore new technology products online.

Key stages include:

- Discovering the product
- Learning about features
- Comparing customization options
- Deciding whether to purchase

This helped shape the structure of the website and the product exploration flow.

![User journey](/images/visiontalk-journey.jpg)

## Design Process

The design focused on creating an interactive and visually engaging experience.

Key interaction elements include:

- Parallax scrolling to create depth
- Hover interactions to reveal product details
- Zoomable product components
- Theme adjustments based on context

Wireframes and prototypes were developed to test navigation and product exploration flow.

![Wireframes](/images/visiontalk-wireframe.jpg)

## Solution

The final design provides a guided product discovery experience that helps users understand the capabilities of smart glasses.

Key features include:

- Interactive product showcase
- Customizable frame and color options
- Clear visual presentation of product features
- Structured shopping interface

![Final UI](/images/visiontalk-ui.jpg)

## Reflection

This project explored how interaction design can make complex technology easier to understand.

Designing the website helped me learn how motion, layout, and visual storytelling can guide users through a product discovery journey.
